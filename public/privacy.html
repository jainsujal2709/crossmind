Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

CrossMind is a classroom learning platform. It turns study material into crosswords and quizzes and lets teachers run quizzes for their students. This page explains what information the platform keeps and how it is used.

Information we keep

  • Account details: your name, email address, role (student, teacher or administrator) and a password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text.
  • Optional profile details: for students, roll number, course, division and phone number. For teachers, employee ID, department, subject and phone number, entered by the administrator.
  • Learning activity: classrooms you joined, quiz answers, scores, time taken, crossword progress and your last sign-in time.
  • Material you provide: text extracted from files you upload, notes you paste, topics you enter, and the crosswords and quizzes generated from them. Teachers can review these in History.
  • Technical logs: error events that help the administrator fix problems. Passwords are not written to logs.
  • Sign-in protection: failed sign-in attempts are recorded with the email entered and the network address for up to one day, to block password guessing.
  • Sign-up source: if you arrive through a link with campaign tags (utm_source and similar), those tags are saved with your account when you register.

Browser storage

When you sign in, the site sets one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read it, and it is sent only over secure connections. It expires after 3 hours, after which you are signed out automatically. Your browser's local storage also holds your display name, role, sign-in expiry time, theme choice and your consent choice. Your browser keeps copies of our style and script files (cache) so pages load faster. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party scripts.

How information is used

We use it to sign you in, build and grade quizzes and crosswords, show results and leaderboards, and let administrators manage accounts. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertisements.

Who can see it

  • Teachers can see the results of students in their own classrooms.
  • Classmates can see leaderboard entries according to the privacy option the teacher chose for each quiz.
  • Administrators can see and manage all accounts.

Services that handle data for us

  • Hosting and database: the site runs on Vercel and stores data in a Neon PostgreSQL database.
  • Topic lookup: when a teacher enters only a topic, that topic text is sent to Wikipedia to fetch reference text. Nothing else is sent.
  • Voice input: the microphone button uses your browser's speech recognition. Depending on the browser, audio may be processed by the browser vendor. Voice input is optional.

Keeping and deleting data

Information is kept until the account is deleted. To correct or delete your information, ask your teacher or the CrossMind administrator. Administrators can edit, disable or delete any account. Deleting history items removes them from the teacher's History page.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and the server checks your role on every request. No online service can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong password and sign out on shared computers.
